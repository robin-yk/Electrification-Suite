Table S7. Questions the solver answers, and the boundary of each answer

Intended as Supporting Information Table S7. Every limitation below is a property of the model as implemented at solver commit 2d93fad, not a statement about what a Joule-heated reactor does.

QuestionSupportedLimitation
What steady temperature does an element reach at a given current or power?YesGrid dependence must be checked. The default 30 × 60 mesh sits 0.60 K below the Richardson-extrapolated average, which is 0.07 % of the temperature rise.
How does temperature vary along the element and across its radius?YesAxisymmetric. Azimuthal variation, and any asymmetry in the enclosure or the flow, is outside the formulation.
Where in the element is the electrical power deposited?YesThe field solve returns a distribution only. Its integral is renormalised to the scalar total, so a local hot spot redistributes power but cannot increase the power drawn.
How quickly does the element heat and cool under a pulsed drive?YesBackward Euler, first order in time and unconditionally stable. A step size that is stable is not automatically accurate, so the step must be refined and compared.
How is the heat lost, between radiation, convection and gas enthalpy?YesElement-to-wall exchange is a single grey-body pair. The gas is transparent; no participating-medium radiation is computed.
What does a rectangular element do?YesSolved through a cylinder that preserves length, radiating surface, mass and resistance. Geometric volume is not preserved, so any quantity read directly from volume is not meaningful.
How does the design scale to a multi-element reactor?In partOne element in one enclosure. Element-to-element radiation, manifold distribution and electrical balancing across a bank are outside the model.
What chemistry does the process gas undergo?NoThe gas carries sensible enthalpy only. No reaction, no species transport, no heat of reaction.
What is the flow field in the purge gap?NoA specified mass flow with plug-flow enthalpy transport. Momentum is not solved, and no velocity profile, pressure drop or recirculation is produced.
Will the element fail, oxidise or drift with age?NoCurrent density is reported against a stated material limit. There is no damage, oxidation or property-drift model.

Colour and type conventions

One hue is assigned to one physical role and is used the same way in all five figures. The set is colourblind-safe and stays legible in greyscale because the roles are also distinguished by position and by line style. Type is Arial throughout, no lighter than 8 pt at the printed size, on white with no shadow, gradient or transparency.

RoleHueWhere it appears
Scalar electrical#0072B2The branch that returns one number: volume-average temperature, series resistance, current, total power.
Local electrical field#E69F00The unit-potential solve and the normalised heating distribution it returns. Also the element region in the mesh.
