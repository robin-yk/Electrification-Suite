Table S1. Fitted correction and development criteria

What the plate that used to sit here reported, as a table. The five length scales are given for reproducibility, not as a ranking of physical importance: the inputs are coupled through the element energy balance, so a short length scale says the fit varies quickly along that standardised coordinate, not that the conversion depends causally on that variable. Every value is read from the same fitted bundle the browser loads. All five criteria were prescribed before fitting; four of them are reapplied in the independent evaluation of Fig. 4, because the long-period consistency check is a development criterion only.

QuantityValueNote
Model scope. The reacting gas is assigned the element temperature, and reaction heat is not returned to the element balance, so conversion cannot feed back on temperature and thermal runaway is outside the model. Element-scale gradients, surface chemistry and material degradation are outside it as well: no layer carries oxidation, sublimation or a lifetime model, so a temperature the element reaches here is not one it necessarily survives. The steady plug-flow calculation is used for comparison rather than prediction, and no absolute yield is claimed.
